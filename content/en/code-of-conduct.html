Code of Conduct

Do onto others as you would have them do unto you

Respect and Non-Discrimination

A respectful behaviour is important and is the basis of each factual discussion. This applies not only offline but also online. It is important to us that all users can express themselves without being discriminated against based on their person, origin or views. Posts that do not adhere to these guidelines will be deleted.

We obey the law, please do that too

We comply with German law; this applies especially to data protection, equality and non-discrimination. Content that violates applicable law will be deleted.
